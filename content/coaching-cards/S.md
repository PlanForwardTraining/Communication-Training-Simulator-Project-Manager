# High S — Steady

> Your client is calm on the surface but deeply unsettled by change. They want stability.

- Slow down. Don't rush this conversation
- Acknowledge the disruption sincerely before moving to solutions
- Walk them through what happens next, step by step
- Be consistent — follow through on everything you say
- Never pressure them for an immediate decision

**Their fear:** Instability and broken trust. Be steady and predictable.
