# Coaching Cues — All Calls

- **Nail the opening.** State the purpose, agenda, and outcome.
- **Smile and speak with intention.** Cut "ums" and "likes"; speak clearly.
- **Embrace the pause.** Confident silence shows you are thinking.
- **Listen to uncover.** Find the "why" behind every request.
- **Lead with solutions.** Guide clients; don't just present problems.
- **Have fun and be the proactive expert.** Advise on budget and timeline impacts.
- **Nail the close.** Summarize decisions and all next steps.
- **Solidify in writing.** *(auto)* Email the meeting summary immediately.
