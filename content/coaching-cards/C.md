# High C — Conscientious

> Your client is analytical, detail-oriented, and skeptical. They want accuracy.

- Come prepared with data, timelines, and documentation
- Never guess — if you don't know, say so and follow up
- Explain your reasoning, not just your conclusion
- Expect detailed questions — welcome them, don't deflect
- Give them time to process before expecting a response

**Their fear:** Being wrong or making a poor decision. Give them the facts.
