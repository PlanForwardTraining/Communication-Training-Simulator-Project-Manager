# High I — Influencer

> Your client is emotional, relationship-driven, and expressive. They want to feel heard.

- Open warm — acknowledge the relationship first
- Validate their feelings before you talk facts
- Use "we" language — you are on the same team
- Keep the energy positive even when the news is hard
- Reassure them that the outcome will still be great

**Their fear:** Loss of enthusiasm or being dismissed. Keep them engaged.
