# I/S — Warm and Steady

> Your client values relationships first and stability second. They will be polite even when disruption hurts them more than they show.

- Lead with the relationship — ask how they're doing before delivering news
- Slow down once you start the difficult message — pace matters as much as content
- Use "we" and "us" language — they want a partnership, not a transaction
- Acknowledge the disruption sincerely; don't minimize the impact
- Reassure them you'll be there step-by-step through whatever comes next
- Never push for a fast decision — they need time to think it through

**Their fear:** Letting people down, or feeling abandoned by their team. Make them feel cared for and consistently supported.
