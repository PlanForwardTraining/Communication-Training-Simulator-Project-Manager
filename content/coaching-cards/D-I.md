# D/I — Driver with Enthusiasm

> Your client wants results but also wants to feel good about them.

- Lead with the solution but deliver it with energy and confidence
- Acknowledge the relationship briefly — then get to business
- Frame the path forward as a win
- Don't linger on the problem — they want momentum
- Be direct and positive simultaneously

**Their fear:** Losing control and losing face. Give them both a solution and a story they can tell.
