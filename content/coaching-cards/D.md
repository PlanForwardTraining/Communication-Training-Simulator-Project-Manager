# High D — Dominant

> Your client is direct, impatient, and results-driven. They want control.

- Lead with the solution, not the problem
- Be brief. Get to the point in the first two sentences
- Never make excuses — own it and move forward
- Give them a clear decision to make
- Match their energy — be confident, not apologetic

**Their fear:** Being taken advantage of. Show strength and competence.
