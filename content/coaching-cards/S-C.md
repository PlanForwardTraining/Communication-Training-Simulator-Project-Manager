# S/C — Steady with Precision

> Your client is quiet, patient, and processing everything carefully.

- This client will not show you how upset they are — do not assume silence means agreement
- Be thorough and calm — walk through every detail
- Give them space to ask questions without rushing
- Follow up everything in writing
- Consistency and accuracy will rebuild or maintain trust

**Their fear:** Surprises and careless mistakes. Be methodical and reliable.
