# Coaching Rubric — Categories & Weights

> **Status:** Placeholder content. Replace with your company's real rubric before going live.

The PM is scored 1-5 in each of six categories. The weights below convert that to a score out of 100.

| # | Category | Weight | What It Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Empathy & Acknowledgment** | 15% | Did the PM make the client feel genuinely heard before pivoting to solutions? |
| 2 | **Clarity & Honesty** | 15% | Was the message delivered clearly, completely, and without spin or hedging? |
| 3 | **DISC Adaptation** | 25% | Did the PM adapt their natural communication style to fit this specific client's DISC profile? |
| 4 | **Solution Orientation** | 15% | Did the PM offer a clear path forward with options, owners, and dates? |
| 5 | **Ownership & Accountability** | 15% | Did the PM take appropriate ownership without over-blaming, deflecting, or excessive contrition? |
| 6 | **Confidence & Composure** | 15% | Did the PM stay calm and authoritative under pressure, including pushback or emotional escalation? |
| | **TOTAL** | **100%** | |

---

## Why DISC Adaptation Is the Heaviest

DISC fluency is the unique communication advantage the company invests in. Every PM can be trained to be empathetic, clear, and composed — those are baseline professional skills.

DISC Adaptation is the differentiator: the ability to read what *this specific client* needs and shift to meet them, rather than running the same playbook on every client. That's what separates a great PM in this company from a generally competent one elsewhere.

The 25% weight reflects that priority. If the company wants to emphasize a different category, adjust the weights here — just keep the total at 100%.

## Score Calculation

```
Total = (Cat1 × 0.15) + (Cat2 × 0.15) + (Cat3 × 0.25) + (Cat4 × 0.15) + (Cat5 × 0.15) + (Cat6 × 0.15)
        ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                                              5
        × 100
```

Equivalent: weighted average of category scores (1-5), normalized to a 0-100 scale.

**Example:**
PM scores 4, 5, 3, 4, 4, 5 across the categories.
- Weighted sum: (4×0.15) + (5×0.15) + (3×0.25) + (4×0.15) + (4×0.15) + (5×0.15) = 0.60 + 0.75 + 0.75 + 0.60 + 0.60 + 0.75 = **4.05**
- Normalized: 4.05 / 5 × 100 = **81**

That's a strong score in the "Strong — minor coaching opportunities" band.
